General Travel Safety Tips

Documents

Always keep digital copies of your passport, visa, and travel insurance in a secure cloud folder.

Money

Split cash across different pockets. Use a travel card with low foreign transaction fees. Never flash large amounts.

Health

Check vaccination requirements 6–8 weeks before departure. Carry a basic medical kit and any prescription medications.

Connectivity

Buy a local SIM on arrival or get a travel eSIM. Share your itinerary with someone at home.

Scams

Be wary of overly friendly strangers, unofficial taxis, and unsolicited tour guides. Book transport through apps.

Insurance

Never travel without comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical evacuation, cancellations, and lost luggage.
